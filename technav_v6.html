Why these prompts work — the five-part structure

  1. Role — "You are a…" sets the lens the AI thinks through.
  2. Task — "Your task is to…" one job, stated plainly.
  3. Audience / context — who this is for and what stage they're at.
  4. Constraints — what to leave out. This is where quality comes from.
  5. Output format — exactly what shape the answer should take.
